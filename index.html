How to read this

The table shows funding the way each exchange quotes it. Hyperliquid quotes per hour, Binance per funding interval of 1, 4 or 8 hours. Positive funding means longs pay shorts, and negative means shorts pay longs.

Spread per year is the gap between the two hourly rates times 8,760 hours. You short the exchange with the higher rate and go long on the other, so price moves cancel and the gap is what you earn.

Costs are four trades, opening and closing on both exchanges, plus crossing the bid-ask spread on each. Breakeven is how long the spread has to last to pay for them. Net per year is what's left if today's spread holds for the whole holding period. It usually doesn't, so click a coin to see how the last 7 days went.

Flags mark spreads that come from a market in trouble. Extreme funding. One side is paying 0.01% an hour or more, eight times the normal rate. Near cap. Binance funding is at 80% or more of its limit. 1h funding. Binance pays this coin every hour, which usually means funding recently hit its limit. Moved. The price moved 15% or more in 24 hours.

Volume is the smaller of the two exchanges. The 24h volume filter drops coins too thin to trade, except when you search for one by name. Not investment advice. Source code on GitHub.
